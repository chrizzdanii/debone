Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information. Last updated: November 2026.

ExploreTalk to us

DEBONE Photos & Video Editing Studio (“DEBONE”, “we”, “us”) operates this website, the online academy, the shop and the help assistant. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who sees it, and what rights you have.

1. Who we are

DEBONE Photos & Video Editing Studio, 179 Old Refinery Road, Elelenwo, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Phone: 0803 598 8889 and 0813 976 3301. You can also reach us on WhatsApp at 0803 598 8889.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), profile details you choose to add (such as photo, date of birth, location, education and an emergency contact) and a password. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read them.
  • Learning records: the courses you enrol in, lesson progress, notes, live class attendance, assignment submissions, marks and feedback, certificates, and messages and files you send in live class chat and the Community.
  • Payment records: the course or bundle, amount, date and status of each payment. Card details are entered on Paystack’s secure page. We do not see or store your card number.
  • Forms you send us: contact messages, feedback and suggestions, instructor applications, quotation requests, webinar registrations and newsletter sign-ups, including what you write in them.
  • Orders and enquiries: items you add to your order list and the messages you send us on WhatsApp, phone or in person. For NIN and ID services, we handle the documents and details you give us only to carry out the service you asked for.
  • Assistant conversations: the questions you type into the help assistant and its replies.
  • Technical information: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and basic server logs needed to keep the site secure.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and give you access to courses, live classes, assignments and certificates.
  • To process payments and confirm enrolments.
  • To answer your messages, take your orders, quote for work and provide the services you request.
  • To send password reset emails, class reminders and other important service messages, and our newsletter if you subscribed.
  • To improve the website and courses using your feedback.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse.

4. Cookies

We use one essential cookie named dsid. It keeps you logged in and is needed for the dashboard and admin panel to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded content from other companies, such as the Google map on our contact and about pages and videos from YouTube, may set their own cookies when you load or use it.

5. Who we share information with

  • Paystack, to process payments.
  • YouTube or the video room provider (for example Google Meet or Zoom), when you join live classes.
  • Google Maps, which loads when you view our map and may receive your IP address and device details.
  • Our email provider, only to deliver password reset, reminder and service emails.
  • Our hosting and file storage providers, who store the site’s data and uploaded files for us.
  • An AI service provider, if the help assistant uses one: the messages you type into the assistant are sent to it to produce a reply.
  • Your instructors and classmates, who can see your name and what you post in classes and the Community, and your instructors can see your submissions and marks.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and learning records while your account is active. Payment records are kept as required for accounting and tax purposes. Certificate records are kept so that certificates can still be verified. You can ask us to delete your account at any time, subject to those requirements.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, to stop certain uses, and to stop sending you the newsletter (use the contact details below). We will respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe your data has been mishandled.

8. Security

Passwords are scrambled, our connections use HTTPS when hosted, and access to the admin panel is limited to authorised staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and do not share it, including with the assistant.

9. Children

Our academy is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a younger person has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, use Contact & support, or WhatsApp or phone 0803 598 8889 and 0813 976 3301.
