Our story

It started with a camera.

DEBONE began with one belief: Port Harcourt deserves to see itself in its best light. What started as a photo and video editing studio grew, one request at a time, into a place where people shoot, edit, print, repair, equip and learn.

We still work from the same idea. Do the job properly, tell the customer the price before we start, and share what we know. That is why so many of our students and customers keep coming back.

The journey

How the studio grew.

  1. Chapter 01 · 1997

    A studio opens

    DEBONE starts as a photo and video editing studio, with a simple promise to make Port Harcourt look unforgettable.

  2. Chapter 02

    Shooting everything

    Weddings, events, portraits and music videos follow, then drone and aerial coverage for bigger stories.

  3. Chapter 03

    Under one roof

    Printing, computer and printer repair, gear and ID services join the studio, so customers can finish a job in one place.

  4. Chapter 04

    The academy

    Live, instructor led courses turn years of practice into teaching, and students become the next generation of creators.

  5. Today

    Still not done

    The same studio, the same standards, and a team that is still adding new ways to help.
