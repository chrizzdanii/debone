How a shoot runs

From first message to final file.

REC 01 / 04
  1. 01

    Brief & plan

    Tell us what you need on WhatsApp: the occasion, the date and the place. We reply with availability, and agree the shots, timing and price before anything is booked.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    Photo, video or drone, lit and directed properly, in our studio or on location. You will not be left guessing what is happening on the day.

  3. 03

    Edit & finish

    We cull, cut, colour-grade and retouch in-house, with updates along the way so there are no surprises at the end.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    You receive the finished files, sized for the platforms you use, with prints, albums or large-format pieces if you asked for them.
